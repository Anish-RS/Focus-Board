1Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Nudgeboard (“Nudgeboard”, “we”, “us”), a sticky-note to-do application available at nudgeboard.vercel.app, collects, uses and protects personal information. Nudgeboard is operated by an individual developer based in India.

By creating an account or using the service, you agree to the practices described on this page.

2Information we collect

CategoryDetails
Account informationEmail address, chosen username and password. Passwords are processed by our authentication provider and stored in hashed form; we cannot view them.
Your contentNotes, tasks, routines, documents and streak data that you create, stored so they can sync across your devices.
Plan informationTrial end date and subscription status.
Payment referencesIf you subscribe, our payment provider shares identifiers (customer ID, subscription ID) and payment status. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details.
Public feedbackMessages and display names you post on the feedback page are visible to everyone.
Please do not post personal or financial information on the public feedback page.

3How we use your information

  • To provide the service: authenticate you, save and synchronise your board, and apply your trial or subscription.
  • To communicate with you about your account, such as password recovery and payment-related notices. Your email address is never displayed within the application.
  • To improve the service, based on feedback you choose to submit.
We do not sell your personal information, we do not display advertising, and we do not share your information for marketing purposes.

4Service providers

We use the following third-party providers to operate the service. They process data on our behalf and may do so on servers located outside India.

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseDatabase and user authentication
VercelWebsite hosting and server functions
RazorpayPayment processing for subscriptions
Exchange-rate lookup (open.er-api.com)Shows an approximate price in your local currency next to the ₹249 price. This request comes from your browser, so that service sees your IP address. No account data is sent, and the INR price is always the one charged.
Web fontsFonts are served from this site itself, not from Google. No request is made to a third party to render text.

5Local storage on your device

The application uses your browser’s local storage and a service worker so that your board loads quickly and works offline. This information remains on your device. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

6Data retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and associated board data at any time (see Section 10). Certain payment records may be retained where required by law or by our payment provider.

7Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. We will respond within a reasonable period. To protect your account, we may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder.

8Children

Nudgeboard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please let us know so that we can remove it.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date on this page will change when we do. Continued use of the service after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the Feedback page.

Posts on the Feedback page are public. Include only your username and the nature of your request — never your email address, password or payment details.
