Most habit trackers quit on you the moment you do.

You get sick, or you travel, or you're just tired, and the streak breaks. So the app starts to feel like one more thing keeping score against you — and it's usually the app that gets deleted, not the habit. Focus Board doesn't reset when you miss a day. It just waits.

Three things it actually does

Weekly routines

Set what repeats — gym on Monday, laundry on Wednesday, whatever's yours. Pin it once and it's there every week without re-adding it.

Carries over

Didn't finish something today? It shows up on tomorrow's board on its own. Nothing quietly vanishes at midnight.

Streaks that wait

Finish a task and the streak builds. Miss one and it holds where it was instead of snapping back to zero.

Free on one device. Synced if you want it.

The board itself — routines, carry-over, streaks, reminders — is free, full stop. Syncing it across your phone and laptop is the one part that costs money to run, so that's the part that may eventually carry a small price. Pricing for sync isn't live yet.
